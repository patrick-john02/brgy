About Barangay Calamagui

Barangay Calamagui West is a vibrant and growing community located in the municipality of Santa Maria, Isabela. As of the 2020 Census, it has a population of 926 residents, accounting for 3.59% of the municipality's total population. The barangay is home to diverse age groups, with a strong workforce comprising 64.28% of its residents, while youth and senior citizens make up the remaining population.

Strategically positioned at approximately 17.4849° N latitude and 121.7658° E longitude, Calamagui West sits at an elevation of 31.8 meters above sea level. The barangay shares borders with several neighboring communities, fostering close ties with its surrounding areas. With a median age of 24, the barangay boasts a dynamic and youthful population contributing to its economic and social development.

Committed to progress and community welfare, Barangay Calamagui West actively promotes livelihood programs, health services, and local initiatives for sustainable development.

  • Active community programs, including free medical check-ups and training for livelihood opportunities.
  • Focus on disaster preparedness and sustainable development projects.
  • Strong emphasis on education, youth development, and economic empowerment.

The barangay remains committed to fostering a safe and progressive environment for its residents, ensuring access to essential services, and strengthening community resilience for future generations.

Services

Free Health Check-Up

Providing free medical consultations, health screenings, and wellness programs for the community to promote overall well-being.

Livelihood Training Programs

Workshops and skills development training to empower residents with knowledge and tools for self-sufficiency and employment opportunities.

Barangay Clearance

Officially issued document for residents, certifying good standing in the community and often required for employment and legal purposes.

Disaster Preparedness Seminars

Educational sessions on disaster response, emergency evacuation, and community resilience to ensure safety during calamities.

Community Feeding Program

Regular meal distribution to children and underprivileged residents, ensuring proper nutrition and support for those in need.

Waste Management & Clean-Up Drive

Regular clean-up drives and waste segregation initiatives to maintain a healthy and sustainable environment for the community.

Greener Barangay Project

A community-led tree planting and waste management initiative aimed at promoting a cleaner and greener environment.

  • Free tree seedlings for residents.
  • Monthly clean-up drives and waste segregation campaigns.
  • Environmental awareness seminars for households and schools.

Housing Assistance Program

Providing financial and material support for low-income families in need of home repairs and sustainable housing solutions.

  • Subsidized construction materials for qualified households.
  • Collaboration with NGOs and government agencies for affordable housing solutions.
  • Disaster-resilient housing workshops for residents.

Youth Empowerment Initiative

A series of workshops, scholarships, and leadership programs to support youth development and education.

  • Free IT and vocational skills training.
  • Barangay-sponsored scholarships for underprivileged students.
  • Leadership and sports development programs for young residents.

Solar Streetlight Installation

Enhancing community safety and energy efficiency by installing solar-powered streetlights in key areas.

  • Improved visibility and security at night.
  • Reduced electricity costs through sustainable energy solutions.
  • Installation in high-traffic and remote areas to prevent accidents and crimes.
