About Barangay Bangad

Barangay Bangad is a thriving community located in the Municipality of Santa Maria, Isabela. As of the 2020 Census, it has a population of 2,932 residents, representing 11.38% of the municipality's total population. The barangay has a diverse age distribution, with the majority of its residents belonging to the working-age group (15-64 years), comprising 62.25% of the population, while young dependents (aged 14 and below) make up 34.13%.

Strategically positioned at approximately 17.4953° N latitude and 121.7802° E longitude, Barangay Bangad is situated at an elevation of 33.1 meters above sea level. The barangay maintains strong connections with its neighboring communities and continues to play a key role in local economic and social development. With a median age of 21, the barangay fosters a dynamic and youthful population that contributes to its progress.

Dedicated to the well-being and advancement of its residents, Barangay Bangad actively promotes community development programs, economic empowerment, and sustainable initiatives.

  • Implementation of community programs, including health services and livelihood training.
  • Emphasis on disaster preparedness and environmental sustainability.
  • Strong support for education, youth development, and economic initiatives.

Barangay Bangad remains committed to fostering a secure and progressive environment for its residents, ensuring access to essential services, and strengthening community resilience for future generations.
