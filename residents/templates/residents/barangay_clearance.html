About Barangay Clearance

Purposes of Barangay Clearance:

  • Certifies good moral character and honesty.
  • Required for job applications and business dealings.
  • Needed for financial transactions, gun licenses, and legal matters.

Requirements:

  • Completed Barangay Clearance Application Form
  • Proof of Residency (e.g., utility bill, certification from barangay)
  • Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)
  • Barangay Clearance Fee

Validity: 1 year from the date of issuance.

Uses: Job applications, business permits, passport applications, vehicle registration, marriage licenses, and construction permits.
